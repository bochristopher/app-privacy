Privacy Policy · Field Logbook

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Developer: Bo Redfearn
Contact: redfearn14@gmail.com

Field Logbook is a field-work app for logging test runs, notes, photos, videos, and a project timeline on your device.

What we collect

Field Logbook does not require an account and does not have a login. The app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with us or with advertisers.

Data stored on your device

Notes, projects, test-run records, photos and videos you attach, and export files are stored only on your device. If you choose to add a photo or video, the app uses your camera or photo library with your permission. Those files stay on the device unless you export or share them yourself.

No tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not collect identifiers for tracking across apps or websites.

Exports

If you export a backup or share a file, that copy lives wherever you save or send it. We do not receive those files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to Bo Redfearn at redfearn14@gmail.com.

This page applies only to Field Logbook. It does not replace the privacy policy for this website.
