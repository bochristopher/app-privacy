Privacy Policy · Line Clearance Checker

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Developer: Bo Redfearn
Contact: redfearn14@gmail.com

Line Clearance Checker is a reference tool for estimating approach distances near overhead lines, based on published OSHA guidance for unqualified persons, along with related checklists and an optional distance estimator.

What we collect

Line Clearance Checker does not require an account and does not have a login. The app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with us or with advertisers.

Data stored on your device

Any values you enter and checklist progress are stored only on your device.

The optional distance estimator may use your camera and the phone’s motion or tilt sensors, with your permission, to help estimate the height of a line. That camera view and sensor data are processed on your device. We do not receive the camera feed, photos, or sensor readings.

No tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not collect identifiers for tracking across apps or websites.

A note about the tool

The app is a reference aid only. Estimates are approximate and are not a substitute for official OSHA requirements, employer training, or a qualified person’s judgment on a job site.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to Bo Redfearn at redfearn14@gmail.com.
